# Cowthulu Games Wiki

## Who are we?

Cowthulu Games are a group of 4th Year Game Development students currently based in Dundalk.
We are developing **The Dancer** for our final year collaborative project.

**This Wiki contains:**

- Information about us as a Game Development Studio
- Information about our game
- Guides for the team to work on the project
- Notes and Research gathered while developing the project
- Links to Team Resources

## Our Game

The Dancer is a Top-Down Action RPG developed in Unreal Engine 5 about Irish Sign Language.
It aims to introduce and teach players about ISL in a fun interactive environment.
The game is designed to be suitable for gamers aged 7 to 25.
