## About

This document will go into detail about the legal and ethical issues that will arise during the development of **The Dancer**, and what we plan on doing handle each one.

Note: *These are just the initial thoughts from our Concept phase that will likely need revisions in the future.*

## Legal & Data Protection

Due to our game being an educational experience, we aim to track player data and storing it to improve the development of our game.

**Things we will be tracking include:**

- Age Range: 7-13, 13-18 etc.
- Tutorial Completion Duration: How long it took them to complete the intro area
- Sign Success Rate: How many times the failed the sign system before getting the correct one
- Level Completion Duration: How long it took them to complete the full level
- Interactable Interact %: How many of the interactable environment objects they intracted with
- Guide Interactions: How many of the menu guides did they look through

### User Consent

- We don't track anything without the users explicit consent.
- Consent forms will be clearly presented to the user on launch. 
- No hidden data collection, a full easy to read and understand modal.

### Confidentiality

- User data is never shared to third-parties. 
- Everything is kept within the studio and stored securely.
- Data will not be given to LLM's
- Data is used only to improve the educational experience.

### GDPR Compliant

- Our practices of tracking and storing data are GDPR Compliant.
- Parental / Guardian Consent for users under 16.
- Externally Accessible information on all privacy information.
- Collection is anonymous
- Data will be deleted at an institution approved date.

## Ethics & Responsible Practice

While we have good intentions developing a game about Irish Sign Language, it is crucial that we treat the subject with the respect and sensitivity that it deserves.

### Sensitivity 

While we are building a game around ISL, we have to be certain to not just gamify any disability that requires someone to learn how to speak ISL.
Our game is meant to introduce and educate users, not use ISL as a gimmick to make something unique.

### Representation

We will ensure that were are representing Irish Sign Language in a positive light.
The Dancer won't feature any mature content such as gore, innapropriate language and excessive violence.

### ISL Informed

Everyone on the team will be properly educated on everything they need to know about Irish Sign Language and any other related topics represented by our game.
